ML Playground

Train tiny models right in the browser — no server, no build step. Pick a dataset and a model, hit Train, and watch the decision boundary (or regression curve) appear. Click the chart to add your own point.

Press Train to fit a model.

Models are the same dependency-free modules under src/ that the Node tests exercise. Serve this folder with python3 -m http.server and open http://localhost:8000 (ES modules need http, not file://).
